# Identity for AI Agents

Essays on treating service principals, managed identities and AI agents as identities: who they are, how
they prove it without long-lived secrets, who answers for them, and whether they are behaving like
themselves right now. Controls are mapped to Microsoft Entra, SailPoint and Okta.
